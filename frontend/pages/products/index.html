Our Products

LoRa Tracking Collar

Lightweight collar starting at 120 g, designed for large-scale LoRa mesh tracking. Operates for up to 2 years, ideal for defined territories and mass deployments. Location data is uploaded via repeaters or satellite uplink to the dashboard.

Why it’s unique: Ultra-low power, scalable, and cost-efficient for hundreds of animals.

LoRa Repeater

Solar-powered infrastructure node with up to 10 km line-of-sight coverage. One-time installation enables unlimited tracker deployments — as few as 6 repeaters cover ~1,000 km². Ideal for long-term, cost-efficient tracking in defined zones.

Why it’s unique: Minimal infrastructure, massive coverage, zero per-tracker cost increase.

Hybrid Satellite + LoRa Collar

Lead-animal collar with hybrid satellite and LoRa connectivity. Collects data from nearby LoRa trackers and transmits entire herd locations via satellite. Works anywhere on Earth, with up to 15-minute latency and multi-year battery life.

Why it’s unique: Satellite scale without satellite-level costs.

AI Camera Traps

Smart camera traps with on-device AI for species detection, behavior analysis, and poaching alerts. Automatically converts video into structured reports and sends insights via passing animals or repeaters. Fully integrated with Custodia’s tracking ecosystem.

Why it’s unique: No manual data retrieval, no video review, real-time intelligence.
